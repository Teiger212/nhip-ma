## The storyline

Nhịp started from one observed loss: a Japanese, Korean or Russian lead writes a Hà Nội apartment agency on WhatsApp or Zalo and a Vietnamese agent on a phone answers late or never, so the lead goes to whoever replies first. The first build (PRs #1–#7, Aug–early Sep 2026) ported an inbox walkthrough onto the supastarter Next.js kit with a hand-written SQLite store, regex extraction and template replies, and a "kit first" rule for auth, organizations and admin. The 2026-09-06 handoff audit (reports/2026-09-06-handoff-analysis.md) found the inbox routes were unauthenticated and the Zalo secret was never read; PR #10 (fb173be) added requireInboxSession, fail-closed webhook signatures and the first approve-race fix, PR #11 single-sourced the vocabulary in zod, PR #13 deleted Drizzle, PR #16 removed the walk bypass, and PR #18 deepened the inbox (runtime singleton, one adapter per pipe, the mock|live seam). PR #19 wrote the product frame: CONTEXT.md, PRODUCT.md and ADRs 0001–0009 (two roles two screens, funnel over response time, CRM adapter seam, Your turn with no dismiss, AI drafts behind guardrails, reply-only per-message approval, translate every guest message, office is the tenant, build order). Build 1, the conversation loop (ADR 0009, PR #21, shipped 2026-09-18), delivered Your turn, quiet, translation and model follow-ups behind one OpenAI-compatible draft adapter. Build 2 began with office tenancy and Home's empty state (PR #22, ADRs 0001/0002/0008); a read-only GPT-6-Astra audit at that PR (reports/2026-09-20-gpt6-astra-architecture-audit.md) found a forged active-organization exploit, guest merging across offices, replies on global credentials, and three send-contract bugs, which produced ADR 0010 (offices assigned, resolved from membership on every request, ad768a5), ADR 0011 (the Answer as the record of a send, written before the vendor call, PR #23), the funnel counted from Answers in one SQL query (PR #24), the Inbox component split (PR #25) and ADR 0012 moving the inbox from SQLite into the kit's Postgres through Prisma (PR #26, merged 2026-09-21). Where it stands: capture, understand, draft, queue, approve, send, follow up and count are live for the three funnel stages Nhịp can count itself; closings and lost say "Connect your CRM" because the CRM adapter (ADR 0003, Attio provisional plus a mock) is the remaining piece of build 2 and the next step, with listing match as the horizon.

## The 30-second pitch

Nhịp is speed-to-lead for high-end apartment agencies in Vietnam. A lead writes the agency on WhatsApp or Zalo in Japanese, Korean, Russian or English; Nhịp detects the language, extracts what they want, translates it for the agent, drafts a reply in the guest's language and queues it. A human taps Approve and send, and the reply leaves on the agency's own number. Nothing is ever auto-sent. Managers get a funnel on Home: leads in, engaged, in conversation, with closings and lost reserved for their CRM. It is a Next.js 16 monorepo on the supastarter kit, Prisma on Postgres, one vendor-neutral model adapter, and twelve ADRs that say why each rule exists.

## The 2-minute pitch

The problem is a multinational lead writing a Hà Nội agency at 11pm in Korean and a Vietnamese agent who answers the next afternoon, if at all. The lead goes to whoever replies first. Nhịp's wedge is the first reply within minutes; its durable value is a two-way language bridge: every guest message is translated for the agent and every reply is drafted in the guest's language. The guest never sees Nhịp, only the agency's number.

Two roles, two screens. The agent lives in the Inbox, a queue where "Your turn" means the guest spoke last, threads untouched for 48 hours fold into a quiet section, and there is deliberately no dismiss button so the count cannot lie. The manager pays and reads Home, a five-stage funnel counted from a fixed 30-day cohort; Nhịp computes leads in, engaged and in conversation in one SQL query, and closings and lost come only from the office's CRM, never inferred from chat.

The send contract is the part I am proudest of. Every send answers exactly one guest message. An Answer row is written in status "sending" before the vendor is called, unique per inbound, so two agents tapping at once produce a 409 from the database, a vendor refusal can be retried on the same row, and a timeout is frozen as "unknown" and never resent until a person reconciles. That design came out of an outside audit that reproduced a double send in the earlier version.

Tenancy is the office, which is the kit's Organization. The office is resolved from the membership table on every request, never from the session's active organization, because the audit showed that field is client-writable. Webhooks verify vendor HMACs against the raw body and fail closed; a message on a pipe no office has connected is dropped rather than filed under nobody.

The stack is the supastarter Next.js kit, kept "kit first": Better Auth with invitation-only signup and one office per operator, Prisma 7 on one Postgres holding both auth and the eight inbox tables, next-intl with /en and /vi, TanStack Query with a 10-second poll, zod 4 as the single vocabulary, and one hand-written OpenAI-compatible draft adapter so the model is a config change. Regex does language detection and extraction on the hot path; the model only translates and drafts follow-ups in the background, behind a guardrail that drops anything touching Vietnamese paperwork.

It shipped in three builds on a sequence ADR 0009 fixed: the conversation loop, then office tenancy and Home, then the CRM adapter, which is next. Twelve ADRs, a real Postgres in CI, and a test that walks the whole loop and asserts no auto-send path exists.

## What I'd do differently

- Guard the retry path in beginAnswer. The failed-to-sending retry is a plain `update where { id }`; under READ COMMITTED two concurrent retries both read `failed` and both transmit. It should be `updateMany where { id, status: "failed" }` with count 0 treated as in_progress, the same compare-and-set failAnswer already uses. The unique index only protects the first send.
- Count vendor-app replies as engagement. An agent answering from the WhatsApp or Zalo phone app arrives as an oa-echo outbound, clears Your turn, but writes no Answer, so the funnel's `reached` CTE never sees it. An office that half-works from the phone under-reports itself and the manager concludes the tool is unused. Engaged should include oa-echo outbounds with a distinct source.
- Put a unique index on (conversationId, vendorMessageId). Webhook dedupe is a findFirst inside the transaction, so a concurrently redelivered webhook can insert the same guest message twice. Prisma P2002 handling already exists for Answers; the same pattern belongs here.
- Start on prisma migrate from the first Postgres commit instead of db push. There is no production data yet, which is exactly when a baseline is free; deferring it to go-live means the first migration is a large one made under pressure.
- Filter mock Answers out of the funnel, or refuse mock sends on a live database. completeAnswer stores mock: true and the SQL never reads it, so demo sends inflate engaged the day live and mock coexist.
- Write the evaluation set ADR 0005 promised before shipping model follow-ups. The gate for moving the first reply from template to model is 'shown to be better on the invented threads', and nothing measures that; the four seed guests plus a dozen real follow-up shapes would have been a day's work.
- Translate lazily into the reading operator's locale rather than eagerly into both en and vi. ADR 0007 budgets one call per inbound; the code makes up to two, and most offices read in one language. The backfill path already exists, so the eager path is the redundant one.
- Wire Meta's status webhooks to the Answer's vendorMessageId and add a sweeper for orphaned `sending` rows. Today an unknown outcome or a crash mid-send blocks that guest message until someone edits the database; both the data and the parser seam are already there.
- Keep the docs in step with the ADRs in the same PR. Five documents still describe the SQLite era, an Anthropic adapter that never existed, an --adopt-unowned flag that was deleted, and README's claim that organizations are unused scaffolding. Each is a small edit that was skipped at merge time.

## Known gaps to own before being asked

- No CRM adapter. ADR 0003 designs the seam (findLeadForConversation, outcomeFor, listOutcomes, Attio provisional, mock adapter, E.164 phone matching, never name matching) and nothing implements it. Closings and lost show 'Connect your CRM', no thread ever leaves the queue through an outcome, and the 'Resolved' state in CONTEXT.md exists in vocabulary only.
- No reconciliation path for Answers in status unknown. A timeout or a record failure after vendor success freezes the row; the operator sees 409 delivery_unknown until a person checks the vendor log and edits the database. Same for a `sending` row orphaned by a crash.
- The failed-retry race in beginAnswer: two concurrent approvals of a `failed` Answer can both transmit because the retry is an unguarded update by id (verified in packages/database/inbox/store.ts).
- The funnel ignores oa-echo replies and counts mock Answers as sent; the 30-day window is rolling from Date.now() with no office timezone and no picker.
- Vendor credentials are process-wide: one live deployment serves one WhatsApp number and one Zalo OA. A second office's threads are readable but every live reply is 409 pipe_not_configured. PipeConnection carries no token.
- The draft post-check is a keyword list (600-char cap plus a paperwork/visa/ownership regex, substring-matched so 'advisable' and 'co-ownership' are dropped too). An invented price, availability or viewing time passes; human approval is the only backstop. Audit finding #8 is open.
- No evaluation set for model drafts, so the first reply stays a template with nothing measuring whether the model would be better.
- Zalo signature verification enforces no timestamp window, so a captured payload replays indefinitely; vendorMessageId dedupe has no unique index, so a concurrent replay can double-insert.
- Background jobs are in-process promises: no retry, no persistence, no cross-instance dedupe, and a platform that freezes after the response loses them. The app assumes a long-lived Node process.
- Office deletion cascades to threads, pipes and memberships but leaves operator User rows behind with a login and no office (403 no_office). ADR 0012 defers this to a later ADR.
- Nudges are deferred (ADR 0006): a quiet WhatsApp thread is by definition outside the 24h window and cannot be answered from Nhịp at all; the reply box only says so after the tap.
- No rate limiting beyond Better Auth's default memory store, which keys on connection IP until trustedProxies is set; no per-office model metering, the prepaid OpenRouter balance is the only cap.
- Development runs on prisma db push with no migration history; the prisma migrate baseline is owed before the first production deploy.
- Dropped inbounds on unconnected pipes leave only a console.warn, so a forgotten pipe:connect loses guest messages quietly.
- CONVERSATION_INCLUDE loads every message, translation and answer of every thread in the office on every 10-second poll; that query, not the poll, is the scaling limit.

## Contradictions the critic found

- Chapter 'security' (hard question on concurrent approve) says beginAnswer's retry path is not airtight; chapter 'data' says 'one Answer per inbound, always' and ADR 0012 says the transaction plus unique index refuse concurrency. Verified in code: the failed-retry branch is `tx.answer.update({ where: { id } })` with no status guard and no isolationLevel, so the 'data' chapter's guarantee holds only for the first send.
- Chapter 'product' (hard question) and chapter 'data' disagree with the 'product' funnel section: the funnel section says engaged is 'a lead with at least one sent Answer' as if complete, while the oa-echo path (an agent replying from the vendor app) clears Your turn in mapConversation but never creates an Answer, so `reached` never counts it. The code confirms `reached` reads only inbox_answer WHERE status = 'sent'.
- CONTEXT.md:40 defines Your turn as 'the guest spoke last, i.e. there is an unanswered inbound' and CONTEXT.md:54 defines engaged as 'received at least one approved send'; the code says a guest message with an Answer in sending or unknown is not Your turn, and engaged counts only status sent. The Sending section of CONTEXT (line 71) and ADR 0011 have the precise definitions.
- Chapter 'ai' says translation cost is up to two calls per inbound (eager en and vi); ADR 0007 says one call per inbound and that a new locale is translated on first request. Code (translate.ts scheduleTranslations over OperatorLanguage.options) matches the chapter, not the ADR.
- ADR 0006 and ADR 0011 both say 'files from before this decision migrate on open'; ADR 0012 deleted the SQLite store and all on-open migrations. ADR 0010 is annotated as superseded on its point, ADR 0006 and 0011 are not.
- ARCHITECTURE.md:64 says the draft adapter is 'Anthropic or none'; ARCHITECTURE.md:91 and drafts/adapter.ts say `none | openai-compatible`. The chapters 'tools', 'architecture' and 'ai' all flag this; there was never an Anthropic implementation.
- apps/saas/instrumentation.ts:9 says 'The inbox runtime opens SQLite, which only exists on Node'; since ADR 0012 it opens Prisma/Postgres. The Node-runtime gate is still correct.
- HANDOFF.md:128 tells the go-live operator to pass `--adopt-unowned` to pipe:connect; connect-pipe.ts has no such flag and ADR 0012 removed adoptUnownedThreads. HANDOFF.md:13 also names the 2026-09-06 audit as the one that shaped the current code and never mentions the 2026-09-20 GPT-6-Astra audit that produced ADRs 0010 and 0011.
- README.md:40 says organizations and admin are unused kit scaffolding; ADRs 0008 and 0010 make the kit Organization the office and /admin/organizations the place offices are created. README.md:34 says 'Approve claims the thread atomically'; since ADR 0011 the claim is per guest message on Answer.inboundId.
- PRODUCT.md:64 still marks the conversation loop as '(next)'; CHANGELOG shows it shipped 2026-09-18 and office tenancy plus Home shipped 2026-09-20.
- store.test.ts:134 is titled 'your turn is derived from the messages'; ADR 0011 and the store comment say it is derived from Answers, not message order. The test body is correct, the title is pre-ADR-0011.
- The 'tools' chapter and AGENTS.md describe CI as lint, format:check, type-check, test; .github/workflows/ci.yml also runs `pnpm --filter saas seed:check`. AGENTS.md 'Auth & multi-tenancy' says to scope by the active organization helpers, which ADR 0010 and office.ts forbid for inbox and home code.
- The 'tools' chapter says the repo has 38 test files and 132 tests were green at commit 0210ff7; the chapters give no current count and the 'data' chapter cites 13 + 19 + 3 for the three store-backed suites only. Do not quote a total test count in an interview without running the suite.
- Chapter 'security' says the GPT-6-Astra audit recommended 'retain SQLite'; chapter 'tools' presents the Postgres move as the answer to the audit's item 3. Both are true: ADR 0012 resolved the audit's lifecycle concern by a different route than the audit proposed.

## Key facts by chapter

### Chapter 1: The Product — what Nhịp is, who it serves, and the rules it refuses to break

- Nhịp is speed-to-lead for high-end apartments in Vietnam: an inbound on WhatsApp or Zalo becomes a human-approved reply in the guest's language (EN, VI, JA, KO, RU) from the agency's own number.
- Two roles, two screens: the agent (user) lives in the Inbox queue; the manager (customer) reads Home; Home is visible to every operator with no role gating and office-level numbers only (ADR 0001).
- The office is the tenant and is the supastarter kit Organization; threads are shared inside it, one operator belongs to exactly one office, resolved from membership on every request, never the session's active org (ADRs 0008, 0010).
- "Your turn" means the guest spoke last; in code it is `Conversation.unansweredInboundId !== null`, derived in `store.ts:mapConversation` from Answers with status sending, sent or unknown, not from message order (ADR 0011).
- Quiet is a Your-turn thread the guest last touched more than 48 hours ago: `QUIET_AFTER_MS = 48h` in `queue.ts`, a product constant, rendered as a collapsed section at the bottom of the queue.
- There is no dismiss: the queue empties only through sends and CRM outcomes, so the count cannot lie (ADR 0004).
- Reply-only: every send answers exactly one guest message; the approve request names `inboundId` and the exact text; refusals are 409 stale_target, 400 inbound_required, 400 empty_reply, 409 already_answered, 409 delivery_unknown (ADRs 0006, 0011).
- The Answer row is written in status `sending` before the vendor is called; the unique index on `inboundId` blocks concurrent approvals; `failed` may be retried on the same row, `unknown` is never retried automatically.
- Never auto-send is proven by `loop.test.ts`, which walks ADR 0009's done line and asserts every office message is one Answer.
- Nudges are deferred because WhatsApp's 24-hour customer-care window requires paid templates; the WhatsApp pipe adapter refuses free-form sends outside the window (`WA_WINDOW_MS`).
- The funnel is leads in, engaged, in conversation, closings, lost; Nhịp computes the first three in one SQL query in `store.funnel`; closings and lost come only from the CRM adapter, which is not built, so Home shows "Connect your CRM" instead of a zero.
- Cohort rule: `FUNNEL_WINDOW_DAYS = 30` by first inbound; engaged = a lead with a `sent` Answer; in conversation = a guest message after the first sent Answer; response time = first inbound to first `sentAt`, nearest-rank median and p90.
- The seed writes four invented guests into the walk office: Minji (KO, rent, Tây Hồ), Yuki (JA, buy, paperwork flag), Alexei (RU, rent, Ciputra), Thảo (VI on Zalo); logins walk@nhip.local and admin@nhip.local, password walkthrough.
- Build order (ADR 0009): conversation loop (shipped 2026-09-18), then office tenancy plus Home and the Attio/mock CRM adapters (tenancy and Home shipped 2026-09-20; CRM adapter next), then listing match as the horizon.
- The first reply is a regex-keyed template; follow-ups are model drafts from the whole conversation behind one vendor-neutral OpenAI-compatible draft adapter, with a post-check that drops any draft touching paperwork or ownership.

### System Architecture: how a guest message becomes an approved reply

- Only apps/saas ships, on port 3010; marketing, docs, mail-preview, packages/api (oRPC/Hono), payments, notifications, storage and ai are kit scaffolding left unused, while the kit organization (as the office) and the kit admin area are in use.
- One Postgres via DATABASE_URL holds both Better Auth tables and the eight inbox_* Prisma models (ADR 0012); createInboxStore(db) in packages/database/inbox is the only writer, and routes never touch Prisma directly.
- instrumentation.ts validates env once at startup with validateInboxEnv (zod superRefine): SEND_MODE mock|live, live requires the five vendor vars, DRAFT_API_KEY needs DRAFT_MODEL, BETTER_AUTH_SECRET 32+ chars, NEXT_PUBLIC_SAAS_URL absolute; production throws, dev logs.
- getRuntime() lazily builds { store, config, drafts } on globalThis.__nhipRuntime; the draft adapter is openai-compatible (plain fetch, OpenRouter default) or none, and resolveSendMode makes only the exact string "live" live.
- Inbound: raw body -> pipe adapter verifyInbound (WhatsApp HMAC X-Hub-Signature-256, Zalo sha256(appId+body+timestamp+secret), timingSafeEqual, missing secret = 403) -> loose zod parse -> officeForPipe on inbox_pipe_connection (no connection = dropped) -> upsertInbound transaction deduped by vendorMessageId -> regex one-shot -> background translation and model draft.
- Every inbox route is force-dynamic and starts with requireInboxSession: 401 without a Better Auth session, 403 no_office or ambiguous_office from the membership table; the session's activeOrganizationId is never consulted because a user can set it to any org id.
- Thread identity is (office, pipe, guest) with id office:pipe:guest; the store lists and reads strictly by viewer.officeId so a thread is invisible outside its office and shared by every agent in it.
- The Inbox client polls GET /api/conversations?locale= every 10 seconds through TanStack Query; that poll is how new inbounds, translations and model drafts reach the screen, and mutations invalidate ["inbox","conversations"] on success.
- Approve and send checks not_found, already_answered/delivery_unknown, inbound_required, stale_target, empty_reply, the WhatsApp 24h window and pipe_not_configured, then beginAnswer writes the Answer in status sending before any vendor call; the unique index on Answer.inboundId turns a concurrent approval into P2002 -> 409 send_in_progress.
- SendError (vendor refused or missing credentials) marks the Answer failed and may be retried on the same row; any other throw, or a record failure after vendor success, marks it unknown and the app never retries until a person reconciles.
- "Your turn" (unansweredInboundId) is derived in mapConversation from Answers in status sending/sent/unknown or an oa-echo after the message, not from message order, so a guest message arriving mid-send stays in the queue.
- Home is an async server component; funnel.ts is server-only, resolves the office from membership, and store.funnel runs one raw SQL CTE query over inbox_message and sent inbox_answer rows for leads whose first inbound is within FUNNEL_WINDOW_DAYS = 30; closings and lost say "connect your CRM".
- Locale routing is next-intl localePrefix "always" with walkLocales [en, vi]; proxy.ts excludes api, webhooks, dev, image-proxy, _next, _vercel; / is a static next.config redirect to /en/inbox; /de/inbox is notFound even though packages/i18n lists de, es, fr.
- Client state is split by kind: server data in TanStack Query, view and search in the URL via nuqs useQueryState, selection/detailOpen/sendError and reply edits (keyed by unansweredInboundId) in useState inside the Inbox shell; no Zustand or context because nothing outside the shell reads it.
- PR #25 split a 642-line Inbox.tsx into a 176-line shell over InboxToolbar, ThreadList, ThreadRow, ThreadDetail, ThreadMessage, ExtractFields, ReplyBox, SendBar and ThreadParts; every non-rendering rule (queue, search, send status, extract rows) lives in lib/ with a test.
- Tests run serially (fileParallelism: false) against supastarter_test, pushed once by vitest.global-setup.ts and truncated per test; CI runs a postgres:16 service plus lint, format:check, type-check, test and seed:check.

### Data model and the store: eight inbox_* tables, one Answer per guest message, one seam

- Eight inbox models live in packages/database/prisma/schema.prisma, mapped to inbox_conversation, inbox_message, inbox_translation, inbox_qualification, inbox_draft, inbox_paperwork, inbox_answer and inbox_pipe_connection, in the same Postgres as the kit's auth tables (ADR 0012).
- The office is the kit's Organization; Conversation.officeId and PipeConnection.officeId cascade-delete from it, Answer.operatorId set-nulls from User, and everything under Conversation and Message cascades.
- A thread's identity is the unique triple (officeId, pipe, guestId); the id string is office:pipe:guest only because it sits in every route, and upsertInbound looks threads up by the triple, never by parsing the id.
- The office is resolved from the membership table on every request (resolveOffice: 0 → 403 no_office, >1 → 403 ambiguous_office); the session's active-organization field is never trusted because a user can set it (ADR 0010).
- Closed vocabularies are Prisma enums (Pipe, MessageDirection, MessageSource with oa_echo on disk, DraftSource, AnswerStatus); languages and rentOrBuy are text validated by zod in packages/database/inbox/schema.ts, the single source of the vocabulary.
- An Answer is one row per guest message (inboundId @unique) carrying status sending → sent | failed | unknown, written in status sending inside beginAnswer before transmit is called (ADR 0011).
- failed is retried on the same row; unknown (network failure, timeout, or vendor success the app could not record) is never retried and blocks with 409 delivery_unknown until a person reconciles it.
- Your turn (unansweredInboundId) is computed in mapConversation on every read: the latest inbound is answered only if an Answer in sending/sent/unknown exists for it or an oa-echo outbound follows it; message order is not consulted.
- The concurrent-approve race is closed by the unique index, not by transaction isolation: the losing insert fails with Prisma P2002, which isUniqueViolation turns into reason in_progress and the route into 409 send_in_progress.
- completeAnswer throws unless the Answer is sending; failAnswer and markAnswerUnknown are updateMany guarded by status = sending, so a late transition on a sent Answer is a no-op.
- The funnel is one $queryRaw with CTEs first (MIN(at) of inbound per thread) and reached (MIN(sentAt) of sent Answers), cohorted by first inbound >= since inside the office; only the nearest-rank median and p90 (sorted[ceil(p·n)−1]) are computed in JavaScript.
- InboxStore in packages/database/inbox/types.ts is the seam: routes call getRuntime().store, built once as createInboxStore(db) on the kit's Prisma singleton, and tests replace it with setRuntimeForTests.
- The inbox moved from a better-sqlite3 file with hand DDL in ensure-schema.ts to Prisma 7.9.1 on Postgres in PR #26 (five commits, ADR 0012) because the SQLite file had no relations to the office, a second schema tool, and tied the app to one process on one disk.
- Tests run against supastarter_test (TEST_DATABASE_URL or DATABASE_URL + _test, never DATABASE_URL itself), pushed once by vitest.global-setup.ts with prisma db push, truncated per test with TRUNCATE ... CASCADE, with fixture offices and operators upserted because of the foreign keys, and with fileParallelism: false.
- Message and Answer ids are cuid2 generated in the store (not @default(cuid())), with an autoincrement seq column as the ordering tiebreak that replaced SQLite's rowid.

### Drafting, translation and the model seam

- `oneShot()` in draft.ts is synchronous and free: detectLanguage + extractFromInbound + a template reply; it runs over all guest messages of the thread joined by newlines (store.guestInboundText).
- Language detection is an ordered Unicode-block chain: Hangul -> ko, kana -> ja, Cyrillic -> ru, Vietnamese diacritics or word list -> vi, else en; guest languages are en|vi|ja|ko|ru, operator languages en|vi.
- The paperwork flag is one regex; when it fires, `PAPERWORK_FLAG` says "Do not invent Vietnamese law. Do not promise a pink book / sổ hồng" and the crib appends the localized `inbox.paperworkFlag`.
- The operator note is never stored: crib.ts renders it at read time from the Qualification in the operator's locale using `inbox.crib.body`.
- `DraftAdapter` has two methods, translate and followUp, and two providers: `openai-compatible` and `none`; a null result always means the fallback stands.
- The only model client is a hand-written fetch to `{DRAFT_BASE_URL}/chat/completions` with temperature 0.2, max_tokens 1024 (translate) / 512 (follow-up), a 30s AbortSignal timeout, zod-checked response and no SDK.
- Env: DRAFT_API_KEY unset means no model; DRAFT_MODEL is required with a key (no default so it cannot go stale); DRAFT_BASE_URL defaults to https://openrouter.ai/api/v1 and the prepaid OpenRouter balance is the budget.
- Translations are stored per message per operator locale in `inbox_translation` (PK messageId+locale); at ingest a guest message is translated into both en and vi unless it is already in that language; GET /api/conversations?locale= backfills what is missing.
- The follow-up model draft runs only when the guest writes back after a send (`sentAt` set) and a provider exists; the first reply stays a template unless the operator hits Regenerate.
- `generateModelDraft` re-reads the conversation and refuses to write if `unansweredInboundId` changed, so a slow draft never overwrites the next inbound's template.
- `checkFollowUp` drops a draft that is empty, over 600 chars, or matches the paperwork/ownership/visa vocabulary in five languages; substring matching makes it deliberately over-eager.
- Guest text and the guest's profile name pass through `asData`, which strips any <guest_message>/<guest>/<agent>/<conversation>/<facts> tags, and both system prompts declare the tagged content untrusted data.
- `runInBackground` tracks jobs in a module-level Set; `settleBackgroundWork()` awaits until it is empty; failures are console.warn only because the template/no-translation fallback is already in place.
- The UI learns about translations and model drafts by polling every 10 seconds (POLL_INTERVAL_MS in inbox-queries.ts); ReplyBox labels the box by `draft.source` (template|model) and has Regenerate.
- Nothing in drafts/, translate.ts or the draft route can send; sending is only approveAndSend, which writes an Answer before any vendor call.

### Tooling choices and why: the kit, the seams, and what each one costs

- The repo is the supastarter Next.js kit (root package name `supastarter-nextjs`); "kit first" means auth, organizations, invitations and admin are the kit's flows with config flags and one hook, and the office is the kit `Organization`.
- Signup is invitation-only: `enableSignup: false` plus the kit's `invitationOnlyPlugin`, which rejects `/sign-up/email` without a pending invitation; invited users auto sign in because the invitation already verified the email.
- A `hooks.before` middleware in `packages/auth/auth.ts` refuses `/organization/accept-invitation` with `ONE_OFFICE_PER_OPERATOR` when the user already has any membership (ADR 0010).
- Better Auth `baseURL` and the single trusted origin come from `NEXT_PUBLIC_SAAS_URL`; startup validation refuses a disagreeing `BETTER_AUTH_URL` and refuses `AUTH_TRUSTED_ORIGINS` in production; rate limiting is Better Auth's default memory store, not configured in code.
- oRPC runs behind one Hono app mounted at `apps/saas/app/api/[[...rest]]/route.ts`; it serves six kit modules and has no inbox router.
- Inbox, webhook and dev routes are plain Next route handlers gated by `requireInboxSession` (401 no session, 403 `no_office` / `ambiguous_office` from the membership table), because they need raw bodies, signatures and office resolution that `protectedProcedure` does not provide.
- Prisma 7 uses the `prisma-client` generator into `./generated` with `engineType = "client"` and `@prisma/adapter-pg`; `prisma.config.ts` supplies `DATABASE_URL`; `db` is a lazy Proxy singleton.
- Since ADR 0012 the inbox tables (`inbox_*`) share `schema.prisma` with the auth tables; `Conversation.officeId` cascades from `Organization`, `Answer.operatorId` set-nulls from `User`, the unique key is (officeId, pipe, guestId), and `createInboxStore(db)` is the only writer.
- Development uses `prisma db push`; a `prisma migrate` baseline is a go-live checklist step; there are no migration files in the tree.
- next-intl uses `localePrefix: "always"` with locales `en` and `vi` only; `proxy.ts` excludes `api`, `webhooks`, `dev`, `image-proxy`, `_next`, `_vercel`; `/` is a static redirect to `/en/inbox`.
- `packages/ui` is shadcn-style on Base UI (`render` prop, no `asChild`) with Tailwind 4 CSS-first theme in `tooling/tailwind/theme.css`; oxfmt sorts Tailwind classes against that stylesheet.
- Inbox server data lives in TanStack Query under `["inbox","conversations", locale]` with a 10 second `refetchInterval`; the view and search live in the URL through nuqs `useQueryState`.
- zod 4 schemas in `packages/database/inbox/schema.ts` are both validator and type (`export const Pipe` merged with `export type Pipe`); adding a pipe is a one-line edit, proven in commit `0210ff7`.
- pnpm 11 with a version `catalog:` and `minimumReleaseAge: 1440`; Turborepo orders `generate` before build/test; oxlint (type-aware via tsgolint) and oxfmt replace ESLint and Prettier; TypeScript 7.0.2 needs `experimental.useTypeScriptCli` in Next.
- CI runs one job with a `postgres:16` service: install, lint, format:check, type-check, vitest (real `supastarter_test` database pushed by a global setup, files serial), and `seed:check`; no build, no Playwright.
- `SEND_MODE` is the mock/live seam: `resolveSendMode` returns `live` only for the exact string `live`; `transmit()` returns a mock result otherwise; live requires all five vendor secrets at startup and both webhooks fail closed without their secret.

### Security, Tenancy and Operations

- requireInboxSession returns 401 with no session and 403 with error no_office (zero memberships) or ambiguous_office (more than one); every inbox route handler and /dev/inbound starts with it.
- The session's activeOrganizationId is never consulted for access because it is copied from user.lastActiveOrganizationId, a client-writable additional field; the audit's critical finding #1 exploited exactly that.
- resolveOffice reads the Member table on every request and is shared by the API gate and Home's server component, so a revoked membership takes effect on the next request.
- Store reads are viewer-scoped: listConversations filters by officeId and getConversation returns null for another office's thread, so cross-office access is a 404.
- Thread identity is (officeId, pipe, guestId) with a unique index and id office:pipe:guest; the same guest at two offices is two threads (audit finding #2).
- Both webhooks go through handleInboundWebhook, which reads the raw body, verifies the vendor signature with a timing-safe compare, and returns 403 before parsing; a missing WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET or ZALO_OA_SECRET_KEY rejects every inbound.
- WhatsApp signs X-Hub-Signature-256 as HMAC-SHA256 of the body; Zalo signs X-ZEvent-Signature as sha256(app_id + body + timestamp + OA secret).
- Inbound on a pipe with no PipeConnection row is dropped with a warn line and a 200; connections are set by pnpm --filter saas pipe:connect and cascade-delete with the office.
- SEND_MODE is live only for the exact string "live"; transmit returns a mock result for anything else regardless of credentials, and the env schema rejects any third value.
- instrumentation.ts validates env once per Node server, throws in production and only logs in dev; live requires five vendor secrets, and BETTER_AUTH_SECRET must be 32+ chars and not the example placeholder.
- A live reply is refused with 409 pipe_not_configured when the last inbound's pipeExternalId is not WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID (or ZALO_OA_ID when set), because credentials are process-wide in the pilot.
- WhatsApp free-form sends outside 24h of the last guest inbound are 409 outside_24h_window; Nhịp does not invent templates; Zalo's window is always open.
- Sign-up is invitation-only (enableSignup false plus the invitation-only plugin), accepting a second office's invitation throws ONE_OFFICE_PER_OPERATOR, and only a user with role admin can create offices in /admin/organizations.
- Seed logins walk@nhip.local (member) and admin@nhip.local (owner, role admin), password walkthrough, office walk-office; remove them from any shared database before go-live.
- Go-live: create Meta, Zalo OA, OpenRouter, Resend accounts; set the secrets and ZALO_OA_ID; connect each pipe; baseline the schema with prisma migrate instead of db push.
