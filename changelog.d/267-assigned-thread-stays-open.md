## 2026-10-08 (an assigned thread stays open)

### Fixed

- **A manager's assigned thread no longer vanishes from the panel** (#267). Giving the open thread to an operator, from its header or its row's "Assign to…", leaves it open though it left the view, with a toast "Assigned to <name>". Picking another thread, switching view, searching or filtering lets the panel follow the list again; a send still moves on to the next waiting guest.
