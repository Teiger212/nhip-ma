## 2026-10-11 (Stay length in the guest details)

### Added

- **A "Stay" row in the guest details** (#317). Nhịp now reads how long a guest wants to stay
  from their own words ("3 nights", "monthly", "1 year", "6–12 months", in EN, VI, KO, JA and
  RU) and shows it in the office language, after the move-in. It is never guessed: a price per
  month or a move-in date is not a stay. A missing stay length is not added to the Missing asks.
