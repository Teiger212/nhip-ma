## 2026-10-11 (Navy on Marble)

### Changed

- **Nhịp has a look of its own: Navy on Marble** (#314; Eyal, 2026-10-11). Navy ink on a cool
  marble canvas, white paper for content, and one raised card for the open thread. Signal Blue
  now fills only Approve and send and what is selected; Regenerate is navy. Buttons, fields and
  tabs have 8px corners and panels 16px. The type is Manrope, with its Vietnamese subset. Every
  screen follows the new theme, settings and admin included.
- **The turn reads at a glance.** A navy "Your turn" plate, an outlined "Waiting" plate with a
  clock, and a bare "Sent" with a check. Each guest is marked by their language (KO, EN, JA…):
  filled for your turn, outlined while waiting, muted once sent. Who sees which threads is
  unchanged.
- **Rows show the guest's facts as tags.** Under each preview: their language, the area, beds
  and budget, when Nhịp has them. Each value keeps one colour everywhere; an area is an outlined
  tag with a pin in its own earth colours, so it never shares a colour with a language.
- **The guest details open on a stay card.** The area is the title, rent or buy and beds sit
  under it, then the move-in and the budget. A fact the guest hasn't given shows as a dashed
  open slot, and the Missing row still names what to ask for. The details rail is narrower
  (300px), so it now shows at 1440 wide with the sidebar open. On a phone or a narrow pane the
  stay is one line under the header (area, rent or buy, beds, move-in, then the budget), and
  the guest's facts share the line under it (on a phone, with the CRM status).
- **The English line under the reply box looks like a translation.** It and the line inside the
  office's bubbles now read like a guest message's translation: the translate glyph, then the
  text, with no "In English" or "Translation" label above it. The glyph still says which one it
  is when you hover it.
- **The CRM status reads like the pipe, in the thread header:** a small glyph and "In CRM" or
  "Not in CRM yet" in slate, no longer a badge. With the details open beside the thread, it now
  sits in the header after Your turn, past a thin divider, and the details lose their CRM
  section. An office with no CRM shows neither.
- **The view tabs are slots:** a small label over the full count, with the view you're on
  filled blue.
- **Home takes the same look.** The funnel's figures are bold, Leads by day marks today in blue
  with its bold date, and Waiting now is the one raised sheet, its guests marked by their
  language. A small "Quiet · over 48 h" divider now shows where the quiet guests start.
- **Response time says p90 in plain words:** "9 in 10 answered within 2 h" ("9/10 khách được
  trả lời trong 2 giờ"). The number is the same.
- **Clear the inbox search in one tap.** While the search holds text, an X at its end empties
  it, brings back the whole list and leaves the cursor in the field.
