## 2026-10-11 (The phone thread breathes; plates that say something; readable labels)

### Added

- **On a phone, the stay line opens every fact** (#327). The one line under a thread's header
  ("Tây Hồ · Rent · 2 bed · This Friday · 3 nig…") is now a tap target: it opens a sheet from the
  foot of the screen with every stay and guest fact whole, as the desk's details rail shows them.
  The same goes for a narrow pane on the desk.

### Changed

- **The paperwork warning is a chip** (#327). On a phone or a narrow pane, "Do not invent
  Vietnamese law…" no longer takes three lines above the conversation: an amber "Paperwork" chip
  stands in its place and opens the sheet, where the warning reads in full.
- **A row's turn plate shows only when it says something the tab doesn't** (#327; Eyal,
  2026-10-11). Under Waiting and Unassigned the rows no longer all say "Waiting", under an agent's
  Your turn they no longer all say "Your turn", and under Sent they no longer all say "Sent": only
  the exceptions keep a plate, such as a manager's own thread under Waiting. All keeps every plate,
  and the thread header always shows it.
- **Labels and headings are easier to read** (#327). The small capitals in the stay card (MOVE-IN,
  STAY, BUDGET) and Home's funnel stages go from 10px to 11px, with room for Vietnamese marks
  (NGÂN SÁCH). Home's panel headings and the details' Guest and Owner are a size up from the text
  under them.
